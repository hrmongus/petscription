# Product Line

## Naming architecture
House brand + **numbered recipe** + editorial name. The number is how staff and
repeat buyers talk ("we're on 03 now"); the name is what makes someone pick up
the bag. Never one without the other.

`HOWL & OATS · NO. 03 · COLD FRONT`

The job the recipe does — sensitive stomach, senior joints, small breeds — is
printed in Space Mono under the name, plainly. The poetry is in the name; the
information is never in the name.

Each bag carries **one** accent color and no other. The range only looks like a
range when it's lined up on a shelf — which is the point.

### Why these names
They describe the *dog in that situation*, never the ingredient or the benefit.
A senior dog is an elder statesman. A small dog is small talk. A dog with a bad
gut is rummaging around in the understory. It keeps the line from sounding like
a supplement catalogue, and it gives the copywriter somewhere to go.

## Limited runs
One or two a year, numbered in the same series, sold until the run is gone.
- **NO. 07 · LATE HARVEST** — venison, squash, rye. Autumn, ~2,000 bags.
- **NO. 08 · MUD SEASON** — herring, oat, nettle. Late winter.

Limited runs are where we take the risks — an odd protein, a strange grain, a
one-off color outside the palette. The core six never move.

## Beyond kibble
- **THE GARNISH** — freeze-dried liver crumb, in a 90 g tin. For the fussy.
- **HOUSE BROTH** — bone broth concentrate, poured over. Sold with a small tin
  measure. Half the reason to buy it is the ritual, and we don't pretend otherwise.
- **THE SPARE SCOOP** — stamped steel scoop, 120 g calibrated. Free with a 12 kg
  bag, sold separately for the price of a coffee.

## Sizes and format
1 kg (trial / small breed) · 5 kg (the standard) · 12 kg (subscription).
The 12 kg ships in a plain kraft carton with the roundel in one color and
nothing else — the bag inside is the branded object, not the box.

## Subscription — "The Standing Order"
Named after what it is. Six weeks by default, adjustable by the day. Every
shipment carries the batch card for that run. Cancel from the card, no login,
no retention offer, no "are you sure your dog won't miss us."
