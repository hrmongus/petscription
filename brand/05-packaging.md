# Packaging

## The object
A **flat-bottom kraft stand-up pouch** with a tin tie at the neck — a coffee bag
built for 5 kg, not a plastic sack with a picture of a retriever on it. Matte
uncoated kraft outside, food-safe barrier liner inside, a recyclable mono-material
structure where the market allows it. It stands on a counter without apology,
which is the whole packaging strategy: **the bag must not need to live in a cupboard.**

## Front panel (5 kg, 220 × 340 mm face)

| Zone | Content | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top, centred | Roundel, one color, 48 mm | Recipe accent |
| Upper third | `HOWL & OATS` stacked wordmark | Ink |
| Middle | `NO. 03` over `COLD FRONT` | Instrument Serif, 42 mm cap |
| Under name | `SALMON · KELP · FLAX · BARLEY` | Space Mono, 8 pt, +0.09 em |
| Under that | The job, one line: `FOR SKIN & COAT` | Space Mono, 7 pt |
| Lower third | Die-cut **crescent window**, 60 mm, showing the actual kibble | — |
| Foot | `5 KG (11 LB) · COLD-PRESSED · MILLED IN VERMONT` | Space Mono, 7 pt |

The window is the crescent from the roundel, cut through the kraft. It shows the
product, it repeats the mark, and it means we can never quietly make the kibble
uglier.

## The stamp block
Bottom right of the front panel, letterpress-style, in a 2 pt ruled box —
**genuinely variable data**, inked per run, not printed decoration:

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│  BATCH   0412               │
│  MILLED  08 · 04 · 26       │
│  BEST BY 08 · 10 · 27       │
│  RUN     400 KG / 96 BAGS   │
│  → howlandoats.com/b/0412   │
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

This block is the brand's single most important asset. It is the proof behind
every claim in the strategy, and it is the reason the bag is worth 30% more than
the bag beside it. It never gets prettified, shrunk below 7 pt, or moved to the
back.

## Back panel
Reading order, top to bottom, with a hairline rule between each:
1. **The manifesto** (see `02-verbal-identity.md`). Instrument Serif, 14 pt, ragged right.
2. **The full ingredient deck** — Space Mono, 8 pt, every ingredient named, farm
   named where we have it. No "meat meal", no "animal by-product", no "natural flavour".
3. **Guaranteed analysis** — plain table, hairline rules, no shading.
4. **Feeding guide** — by weight, in a two-column grid, plus one honest line:
   *"Your dog is not the average dog. Start here and adjust in a fortnight."*
5. **AAFCO statement, contact, recycling mark.** Small, correct, unhidden.

## Print specification
- **Stock:** 90 gsm natural kraft laminate, uncoated face.
- **Inks:** two spot colors maximum per SKU — Ink, plus the recipe's accent.
  No process build, no gradients, no varnish, no foil except the tin tie.
- **Kill the fifth color.** If a bag needs a third ink to work, the layout is wrong.
- **Window:** die-cut crescent, matte anti-scuff film, registered ±1 mm.
- **Stamp block:** flexo overprint at fill, per run.

## The rest of the system
- **Tin tie:** unbleached paper over wire, roundel embossed at 18 mm.
- **Seal sticker:** 40 mm roundel on gummed kraft. Closes the shipper, closes an
  opened bag, ends up on a laptop. Cheapest media we own.
- **Shipper:** plain kraft carton, roundel in Ink on one face. Interior printed
  with the manifesto — the one flourish, because it's the first thing seen.
- **Batch card:** 105 × 148 mm, uncoated, one card per shipment. Front: batch
  number set enormous in Instrument Serif. Back: the farms in that run, and one
  paragraph about a thing that went right or wrong at the mill that week.
- **Scoop:** stamped steel, roundel on the handle, 120 g calibration line inside.

## Shelf logic
On shelf, six kraft bags with six accent colors read as **one system with six
notes**, next to competitors shouting in full color. The kraft is the loudest
decision we make, because it's the quietest.
